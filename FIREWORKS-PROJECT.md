# Kinetic House — FireWorks fitness membership template

Demo for a gym or studio. Adaptable to boxing, yoga, Pilates, martial arts, and personal training after replacing programs, imagery, schedules, and pricing.

## Included

One responsive page with program cards, sample weekly schedule, coach-profile module, membership comparison, first-visit guidance, and a local-only trial interest preview. Primary CTA: plan a first visit. Fields: name, email, and plan interest. The demo does not transmit, store, book, or charge. Booking, membership, waiver, and payments require the client's chosen provider, credentials, approved pricing and policies, and end-to-end testing.

## Client inputs

Brand, approved photos, real class formats and schedule, staff names and qualifications, location/hours/contact, plan prices and terms, booking platform, waiver, consent/privacy terms, and approved member reviews.

## Recommended FireWorks pricing

$1,499 initial build, reflecting program/schedule/membership content and a configurable trial workflow. Standard hosting $499/year primary or $59.99/month; domain $20/year. Booking-provider setup from $199; membership or payment integration scoped separately from $299, plus provider fees.

## Hero variations

Coach-led group training is the current image direction. Future options: boxing class, yoga/Pilates studio, or personal training session with each client's licensed photos.
